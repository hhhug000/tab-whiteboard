VERY PRIVACY POLICY
Version: 1.0.0
Last updated: AUGUST 20th, 2026

THE SHORT VERSION

We don't care about hoarding your information. We collect only what we
need to keep the service running and to prevent fraud or abuse. If we
ever need to share something with a third party, we'll tell you clearly
who they are and exactly what's being shared. We do not sell your
information, and we do not give it away. We do our best, reasonably,
to keep it private.

Below is the longer version, for anyone who wants the details.

1. WHAT WE COLLECT

We only collect information that is necessary to:
  - Operate the service and keep it working properly
  - Verify who you are, when needed
  - Detect, prevent, and respond to fraud, abuse, or security issues
  - Meet legal or regulatory obligations, where applicable

We do not collect information "just in case" or because it might be
useful someday. If we don't need it, we don't ask for it.

2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use your information only for the purposes listed above: running
the service, keeping accounts and transactions secure, and preventing
fraud. We do not use your information for unrelated purposes without
telling you first.

3. THIRD PARTIES

Sometimes we rely on outside companies to help us operate (for example,
payment processors, hosting providers, or fraud-detection services).
When that's the case:
  - We will clearly identify who the third party is
  - We will clearly state what information is shared with them
  - We only share what's necessary for them to do their job

We do not sell your personal information. We do not rent it, trade it,
or give it away to third parties for their own marketing or profit.

4. HOW WE PROTECT YOUR INFORMATION

We take reasonable, industry-standard steps to keep your information
secure and private. No system is perfectly secure, and we can't
guarantee absolute protection, but we treat your data with care and
limit access to it internally to what's actually needed.

5. HOW LONG WE KEEP INFORMATION

We keep information only as long as it's needed for the purposes
described above, or as required by law, and delete or anonymise it
when it's no longer needed.

6. YOUR CHOICES

You may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your
information, depending on where you live. Contact us using the
information below if you'd like to exercise those rights.

7. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll let you know
before the changes take effect.
